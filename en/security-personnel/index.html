Questions before starting

Transparent from the outset.

Is a group already available?

The first pilot is currently being planned. Availability, scope and start date will be agreed with the employer partner. This page does not promise staff who are already ready for deployment.

Who determines the required qualification?

The preparation pathway will be agreed with the employer and competent organisations based on the specific duties. Official IHK certificates are issued by the responsible IHK; our preparation does not replace them.

How will employment work?

The planned model involves a direct employment contract between the candidate and the German employer. Legal and organisational requirements and cooperation terms will be clarified before starting.

What costs are involved?

Prices and standard terms have not yet been published. Scope, fees and funding of preparation, documentation and travel will be expressly agreed before cooperation begins.
